Three layers, so nobody depends on a screenshot arriving.

WhatHow

2. Scheduled emailIn Ads Manager Reports, save a report with the columns below, broken down by ad, and schedule it daily at 9AM MYT to Donny's email.
3. WhatsApp by 10AMA screenshot of Ads Manager at ad level, "Yesterday" and "Since launch", with the same columns, plus 1 line: what you changed and why.

Columns

Amount spent, Reach, Impressions, Frequency, CPM, Link clicks, CTR (link click-through rate), Landing page views, Cost per landing page view, Leads (website), Cost per lead, Initiate checkouts (website).

What We Watch

  • Cost per lead is the main number. Landing page views against link clicks tells us if the page is loading for people.
  • Leads against InitiateCheckout should be close to 1 to 1 on the sign-up page. If they drift apart, tell us: it's a site problem, not an ad problem.
  • We match TicketMelon sales to ads daily with the UTMs and share cost per ticket back with you.

08Rules Of Engagement

  • Let the ad set learn for 48 hours before judging it. Pausing a clear loser is fine; editing the ad set resets learning.
  • Pause an ad once it has spent 2x the ad set's average cost per lead with no leads.
  • Budget changes of up to 20% a day are fine. Anything bigger, ask first.
  • Never change the dataset, the optimisation event, the landing page or the URL parameters.
  • New copy or creative goes to Donny for approval first. Same-day answers.
  • If something looks broken (leads drop to 0, the page errors, the ad gets rejected), message Donny straight away.
