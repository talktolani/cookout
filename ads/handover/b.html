03Tracking: Your Dataset, Our Site

Meta won't let a new business share its dataset with a partner for several weeks, so we're doing it the other way round. You create a dataset in your business, and our site sends every event into it (as well as into ours). You see everything in your own Ads Manager and there's nothing for you to install.

Setting It Up

  1. 1Done 30 Sep: you created The Cookout KL (28544942708504204) and it's live on every page of thecookoutevent.com.
  2. 2Done 30 Sep: you sent the Conversions API token. Our server's copy of each event starts as soon as Donny adds it on our side, so sign-ups from iPhones and ad blockers still count.
  3. 3Don't generate a new token for this dataset: it would stop the server copy until we swap it in.
  4. 4Open thecookoutevent.com/preregister on your phone. PageView should show on the dataset's Overview within a few minutes. We'll send 1 real test sign-up so Lead shows as active too.
  5. 5In the ad set pick conversion location Website, dataset The Cookout KL, event Lead. If Lead still shows as inactive, message Donny and we'll fire it straight away.
