Voice

Short, warm, a bit cheeky. Food puns are welcome ("Prepare To Get Messy", "Crispy Tenders. Crispier Fits.", "Bun Intended."). Numerals, not words (4PM, 10 Oct). Contractions always. No em dashes. Buttons and headlines in Title Case.

Audience

Hip hop and streetwear crowd aged 18 to 40 in the Klang Valley: the Afrobeats and Amapiano crowd first, Malay friends and the wider KL mix around them. People who'd go to a day party, a pickleball social or an R&B night.

03Tracking: Our Dataset, Your Ad Account

Our site already sends every event twice: once from the browser pixel and once from our server (Conversions API), with a shared event ID so Meta counts each one once. Leads captured through iOS, ad blockers or slow connections still count. You don't need to install anything. We share the dataset with your business and you select it in the campaign.

Setting It Up

  1. 1Send Donny your Business portfolio ID (Business settings, Business info).
  2. 2We share the dataset The Cookout (1092247383161339) with your business: Business settings, Data sources, Datasets, Assign partners.
  3. 3In your Business settings, open the shared dataset and connect your ad account to it.
  4. 4We also grant your business access to The Cookout Facebook Page (ID 1459305463923066) so ads can run under it. For Instagram placements, select Bunker 1965's Instagram account in the ad's Identity section.
  5. 5In Events Manager, confirm you can see recent PageView and Lead activity on the dataset. Then build.

Meta renames these menus from time to time. If a label doesn't match, the dataset's Settings tab will show the sharing option.
